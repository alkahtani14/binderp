Why Google Business Profile Manager?

Your Google Business Profile is often the first impression potential customers have of your business. A single unanswered negative review, an outdated phone number, or a missing opening-hours update can cost you customers every day.

Yet managing Google Business Profiles — especially across multiple locations — means constantly switching between Odoo, Google's dashboard, email, and your review management tool. This module eliminates that friction entirely.

The result: Your team stays inside Odoo. Reviews are answered faster, profile data is always accurate, and brand-consistent responses are just a keyboard shortcut away.

Core Features

🗺️ Multi-Location Management

Connect unlimited Google Business Profile locations via OAuth 2.0. Each location maintains its own token lifecycle and syncs independently — addresses, phone numbers, business hours, websites, and category data.

⭐ Review Management

All Google reviews appear in Odoo with reviewer name, star rating, timestamp, and full review text. Assign reviews to team members, track reply status, and maintain a full reply audit history per review.

💬 Canned Response Integration

Leverage Odoo's built-in mail.canned.response engine. Pre-loaded shortcuts cover every scenario: 5-star thank-you notes, service-recovery replies, food & beverage, auto service, and more. Agents type :gb.thanks5 and the full reply appears instantly.

🖼️ Media Library

Upload photos and videos directly to Google. Categorise by Exterior, Interior, Product, Menu, Team, Logo, and more. Track publish status and Google view counts for every asset in a beautiful Kanban view.

📣 Posts & Announcements

Create What's New, Event, and Offer posts with calls-to-action. Schedule future publication, set expiry dates, and monitor view and click-through engagement metrics — all from a single Kanban board.

🔄 Automated Synchronisation

Scheduled cron jobs run every 6 hours to sync location data and ingest new reviews. A separate 30-minute cron publishes scheduled posts and marks expired ones automatically. Token refresh is handled transparently.

Canned Response Workflows — Practical Examples

The module ships with 17 pre-loaded canned response shortcuts covering the most common Google review scenarios. Agents can access them in the Reply wizard by selecting from a dropdown or typing a colon-shortcut.

Scenario 1 — Five-Star Review, Immediate Response

★★★★★  "Excellent service from start to finish! The team diagnosed my car's issue within minutes. Highly recommend!" — Ahmed Al-Rashidi
1
Review appears in Odoo as New. Agent opens it and clicks Reply to Review.
2
In the Reply wizard, agent selects gb.thanks5 from the Canned Response dropdown.
3
The reply body auto-fills:
:gb.thanks5 →
Thank you so much for your wonderful 5-star review! We are thrilled to hear you had a great experience with us. Your kind words mean the world to our team, and we look forward to serving you again soon.
4
Agent personalises: adds "Ahmed" and a specific detail about the vehicle diagnosis.
5
Clicks Publish Reply to Google. The reply is sent via API, logged in the reply history, and the review status changes to Replied.

Module Architecture

Component Description
google.business.locationOne record per Google Business Profile location. Stores OAuth tokens, address, hours, and sync metadata.
google.business.reviewIndividual Google reviews fetched via API. Tracks reviewer, rating, text, assignment, and reply status.
google.business.review.replyImmutable reply audit log. Every publish event is a new record preserving author, timestamp, and the canned response used.
google.business.mediaPhotos and videos with category, format, Google URL, and view count metrics.
google.business.postGoogle Posts (What's New, Event, Offer) with scheduling, CTA, and engagement metrics.
google.business.oauth.wizard3-step guided OAuth 2.0 Authorization Code Flow wizard.
google.business.reply.wizardReview reply composer with integrated canned response selector.

Integration Points

  • CRM Menu — Appears as a Google Business submenu under the existing CRM top-level menu. No changes to any existing CRM view, model, or workflow.
  • Odoo Mail — Leverages mail.thread and mail.activity.mixin on all main models. Full chatter, activity scheduling, and follower support on every location and review.
  • Canned Responses — Reads from the same mail.canned.response table used by Odoo Discuss and Live Chat. Canned responses you add here are available everywhere in Odoo and vice versa.
  • Multi-Company — All models carry company_id. Record rules enforce company isolation automatically.
  • Google Business Profile API — All API call sites are clearly marked with inline comments. Drop in your HTTP implementation (e.g. using Python requests) and the rest of the module works immediately.

Getting Started

  1. Install the module from Apps.
  2. Navigate to CRM → Google Business → Locations and create a new location.
  3. Click Connect to Google and follow the 3-step OAuth wizard using your Google Cloud Console credentials.
  4. Click Sync Now to import your location data and existing reviews.
  5. Go to CRM → Google Business → Unanswered Reviews and start replying — use the canned response shortcuts for maximum speed and consistency.
